Read these numbers carefully

  • Average monthly searches include close variants and are rounded. Adding rows does not produce unique local demand.
  • Competition describes the relative number of advertisers. It is not a measure of buying intent or proof that specific businesses run ads.
  • The displayed +∞ is not proof of a demand surge. Check the underlying comparison periods before interpreting it.

Missing bids are not a price estimate

Google describes top-of-page bid ranges using historical bids over the last 30 days. It may omit them when historical bid information is limited. Missing bids do not prove zero ad spend or a zero cost per click.
