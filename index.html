02 / Search plan

Put the budget behind a clear project need.

Begin with one demolition-led campaign. Separate the chosen services into tightly related ad groups so each search leads to a relevant message and quote page. Do not activate every researched service at once.

Initial priority / Brian-approved services

Demolition

Choose the first service groups around the projects Brian wants to quote. Start with exact and phrase terms such as [demolition contractor] and “house demolition”.

House and residential demolition · Mobile home demolition and removal · Barn and garage demolition · Concrete removal · Pool removal
Selective expansion / after qualified results

Earthwork

Test clearing, grading or excavation only when approved service fit and real results justify shifting budget. Keep each service's ad and landing-page message distinct.

Land and lot clearing · Grading · Site preparation · Excavation · Foundation excavation · Trenching

Stay local first

Begin in the nearby areas Brian can serve efficiently, within the approved 60-mile Greenville boundary. Use presence targeting and check actual job-site addresses before qualifying inquiries.

Keep traffic controlled

Start on Google Search only, without Search Partners, Display or Performance Max. Use exact and phrase match rather than broad expansion or competitor-brand bidding.

Make the quote page useful

Show real project photos, the services offered and clear coverage. Ask for the project location, work type and timing. State cleanup or disposal inclusions only if Brian confirms them.

Proposed negative keyword themes

Jobs, salaries and training · DIY and how-to · Equipment rental · Furniture and household trash removal · Handyman work · Services outside Premier One's offering. Review match types and search-term context so exclusions do not block useful project searches. Negative keywords reduce waste; they do not guarantee zero irrelevant clicks.

04 / Budget

A small test with a firm ceiling.

Keep the monthly budget at or below $1,000. Put the available ad spend into one Search campaign instead of spreading it across channels.

$1,000

Monthly budget ceiling

Scenarios assume the full amount is available for media. Deduct any agreed fees or tools before allocating spend. Other costs require separate approval within the ceiling; this page authorizes no spending.

If all $1,000 is allocated to ads, $1,000 ÷ 30.4 is about $32.89 per day. An unchanged $32.89 average daily budget corresponds to $999.86 over Google's standard 30.4-day monthly calculation. This is not a hard daily cap; reconcile billed spend and adjust pacing when budgets change. Google budget guidance.

05 / Launch & review

Measure the work Brian actually wants.

Use a first-month test to learn which searches produce relevant project inquiries. Keep calls, forms, qualified opportunities, quotes and booked jobs separate in reporting.

  1. Approve the scope and spending limit

    Brian selects the initial demolition services, nearby launch area within 60 miles, budget allocation and verified business claims. Agree on acceptable acquisition cost using actual margins and close rates.

  2. Test the customer path before launch

    Prepare the service page, test successful form submissions and call tracking, and confirm calls reach the person handling quotes. Schedule ads when calls can be answered.

  3. Review closely in the first week

    Check spend, search terms, job locations and inquiry quality. Refine negative keywords and pause terms that repeatedly produce irrelevant work. Pause the test if tracking or call routing fails.

  4. Decide from qualified results

    Review at least weekly and at the end of the first month. Compare cost per qualified inquiry, quotes and booked work. Keep what fits; refine or pause what does not. Expand services or geography only with Brian's approval and within the budget ceiling.

Recommended decision: approve a focused demolition-led Search test, with a maximum $1,000 monthly budget and a 60-mile service-area boundary. Start near Greenville and let qualified results guide expansion.
